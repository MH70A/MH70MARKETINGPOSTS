بعد أن استعرضنا ضمائر الفاعل (Subject Pronouns)، حان الوقت لنتعرف على زميلتها المهمة: ضمائر المفعول (Object Pronouns). هي سهلة ومكملة لضمائر الفاعل.

### \*\*Object Pronouns: The Receivers of Action! 🎯\*\*

If Subject Pronouns are the "superheroes" (doers) of the sentence, then \*\*Object Pronouns\*\* are the ones who \*receive\* the action! They tell us \*who\* or \*what\* is being acted upon.

\*\*What are Object Pronouns?\*\*
They are words that replace a noun and act as the \*\*object\*\* of a verb or a preposition. This means the action is happening \*to\* them, not \*by\* them.

\*\*Here are the 7 English Object Pronouns (and their Subject Pronoun friends):\*\*

1. \*\*Me\*\* (أنا - مفعول به)
 
 \* \*\*Meaning:\*\* The action is done \*to me\*.
 \* \*\*Example:\*\* "He saw \*\*me\*\*." (He did the seeing, the seeing happened to \*me\*.)
 \* \*\*Example with preposition:\*\* "This gift is for \*\*me\*\*."

2. \*\*You\*\* (أنت/أنتِ/أنتم/أنتن - مفعول به)
 
 \* \*\*Meaning:\*\* The action is done \*to you\*. (Same form as the subject pronoun!)
 \* \*\*Example:\*\* "I will call \*\*you\*\* later."
 \* \*\*Example with preposition:\*\* "I'm talking to \*\*you\*\*."

3. \*\*Him\*\* هو - مفعول 
 \* \*\*Meaning:\*\* The action is done \*to him\* (a single male).
 \* \*\*Example:\*\* "She helped \*\*him\*\* with his homework."
 \* \*\*Example with preposition:\*\* "Give the book to \*\*him\*\*."

4. \*\*Her\*\* (هي - مفعول به)
 
 \* \*\*Meaning:\*\* The action is done \*to her\* (a single female).
 \* \*\*Example:\*\* "We invited \*\*her\*\* to the party."
 \* \*\*Example with preposition:\*\* "I bought flowers for \*\*her\*\*."

5. \*\*It\*\* (هو/هي لغير العاقل - مفعول به)
 
 \* \*\*Meaning:\*\* The action is done \*to it\* (a single thing/animal). (Same form as the subject pronoun!)
 \* \*\*Example:\*\* "I found the lost key. I returned \*\*it\*\*."
 \* \*\*Example with preposition:\*\* "Don't worry about \*\*it\*\*."

6. \*\*Us\*\* (نحن - مفعول به)
 
 \* \*\*Meaning:\*\* The action is done \*to us\* (the speaker and others).
 \* \*\*Example:\*\* "The teacher taught \*\*us\*\* new grammar rules."
 \* \*\*Example with preposition:\*\* "Come with \*\*us\*\*!"

7. \*\*Them\*\* (هم/هن/هما/لغير العاقل الجمع - مفعول به)
 \* \*\*Subject Pronoun:\*\* They
 \* \*\*Meaning:\*\* The action is done \*to them\* (two or more people/things).
 \* \*\*Example:\*\* "I saw my friends and greeted \*\*them\*\*."
 \* \*\*Example with preposition:\*\* "I talked about \*\*them\*\*."

\*\*Where do they usually appear?\*\*

\* \*\*After a verb:\*\* "He likes \*\*me\*\*."
\* \*\*After a preposition:\*\* "She went with \*\*him\*\*."

\*\*Quick Tip:\*\* If you can replace the word with "I" or "he" (etc.), it's a Subject Pronoun. If you can replace it with "me" or "him" (etc.), it's an Object Pronoun!

Understanding object pronouns makes your English sentences flow smoothly and helps you talk about who is receiving the action. Practice using them in your conversations!
